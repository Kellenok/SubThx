## SubThx 0.1.1

- **Less memory.** Sound is kept as 16-bit samples and, past 30 minutes, in a temporary file; the VAD works a group of chunks at a time. A 7.5 hour audiobook peaks at about 1.5 GB on the CPU and 1.3 GB on CUDA (3.3 and 2.9 GB before). If it is still too much, lower the batch size in the settings.
- **Batch size setting.** In the settings, and `--batch-size` in the CLI. Auto now picks from the free video memory (GPU) or free RAM (CPU), so a smaller card gets a smaller batch.
- **Whisper on DirectML.** Fixed garbled subtitles (the decoder read the encoder's output before it was ready; on a 15 minute clip 72 meaningless cues became 249 matching CUDA's text at 99.8%), and 8 windows per batch instead of 4 when the video memory allows.
- **Speed.** The VAD computes features while the model runs; an anime episode with Kitsune on CUDA takes 2.6-3 s instead of 5-8 s.
- **App.** The model loads by itself once its download finishes.
